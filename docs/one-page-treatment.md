## Treatment Draft

> My game is a competitive two-player tactics skirmish game set in a dark fantasy world afflicted by a supernatural corruption. Each player chooses a faction and commands a group of three units on a 5x5 grid. The factions represent the different groups within the world, such as the Fungal Crusaders, a group of knights that utilize the spores of a fungal hivemind, and the Demonic Horde, creatures that come from a mysterious red smoke, transforming those who come into contact with it into thralls of the horde. Each faction approaches combat differently through the usage of a unique faction ability, encouraging players to change their strategy depending on their chosen faction.
>
> At the beginning of a match, players draw a card that dictates how to arrange the obstacles around the grid, such as trees and boulders, before allowing them to position their units along the opposite sides of the field. Terrain affects both where the units can move and their attacks. Units can't move through these obstacles, and they can provide protection from ranged attacks. Because the grid is intentionally small, these terrain obstacles can significantly affect how each player interacts with one another and create interesting tactical moments within the game.
>
> During each turn, players receive three action points that can be used to activate their units. Units are given a tier representing their relative power, with higher-tier units generally requiring more action points to use. When activated, a unit can move according to its movement value and attack according to its attack range. An attack first requires the player attacking to roll a die against the opposing unit's armor score. If the attack is higher than the armor value, the attack succeeds, allowing the attacking player to roll the listed damage dice for the unit and deal the damage to the opposing unit. Players can decide to then activate another unit if action points remain after the last attack and if they can afford them.
> 
> Each faction also begins the game with three faction ability points that don't regenerate throughout the match and can only be spent up to once per turn. These abilities reinforce each faction's playstyle. For example, Fungal Crusaders can use their faction ability to regen and temporarily reduce the damage of the next attack they take, while the Horde gain benefits from surrounding a unit, allowing them to do additional damage.
>
> The match ends when one player eliminates all of the opposing player's units. This game is intended for players who want to enjoy a shorter competitive strategy game that offers the same customization that other skirmish games have. The physical prototype will focus on having around four initial factions, each with three units. Other pieces such as the grid and unit functionality will also be prioritized. The prototype itself will be constructed by using a cardboard grid, tokens to represent units and terrain, dice for damage and upkeep, and faction cards which show what each faction and its units can do.
