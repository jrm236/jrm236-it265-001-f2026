**Two or three concepts to pitch:**

1. Grid-based tactics game where you build a loadout of three units from a chosen faction and fight against the other player on a 5x5 grid. Factions have their own unique abilities that can be used up to 3 times a game. Players can spend 3 Action points to move and attack with any of their units, increasing the cost needed to use stronger units. The game ends once all of a player's units are wiped out.
2. A card game where players create their own monster using random body cards from a deck. Taking place on a Monopoly-style game board, players can go and fight enemies and players for currency to buy new parts for their monster. After a certain turn number, all players must battle one another; the player with the most points gained from battling other players wins.
3. A game where players are tasked with creating the best spell. This game functions similarly to Cards Against Humanity. Cards drawn can either add new words to the spell name or provide a sentence with blank lines that can be filled with other word cards to dictate what the spell does. Players vote for who they believe has the best card.
