#### Progress
- **What I accomplished**:
  - This week, I played Garfield for the Atari 2600 to complete the Atari Case Study Assignment. Additionally, I continued to iterate upon my design concept. Currently, I have designed the faction abilities for my game.
- **Challenges faced**:
  - When it came to designing the abilities for my concept, I had trouble balancing them without having actually played a prototype version of the game. For example, one ability would allow the player to heal and resist damage of the next attack taken. I wasn't sure what values/dice rolls would allow this to still be powerful without making the unit extremely tanky and unkillable for the opposing player.
- **Solutions**:
  - I wouldn't say that I have solved the potential problem, but when I finalized what I wanted the values to be, I decided that it would be better to initially test with lower dice values. As such, I limited the ability to only heal 1d4 + 1 and reduce the damage of the next attack taken by 1 as well. When testing the prototype, if I see that this ability isn't that strong, I may increase the damage reduction to 2 or change the + to heal value.
