# IT265 Module 2: Concept Pitch Cards

Copy this card for each of your two or three shortlisted concepts. Aim to explain each in about one minute. Use your [idea bank](./01-idea-bank.html) as a starting point.

## Pitch Card

**Working title:**

**Player role and situation:** Players control a group of units from a unique chosen faction and skirmish against another on a 5x5 grid.

**Repeated decision or action:** Players spend a total of three action points to decide which of their units to activate, where to move them, and who to attack for the action. Players can also spend a non-regenerating token to activate their unique faction ability.

**Goal, pressure, or ending:** The goal is to eliminate all of the opposing player's units. Players are limited by their action points, terrain, and faction ability points.

**Hook:** Each faction has different units and a unique ability that allow players to choose their favorite playstyle.

**Smallest useful physical prototype:** This prototype can use a 5x5 cardboard grid, tokens to represent units and terrain, dice for upkeep, and a piece of paper noting the stats and unique ability of each faction and their units.

**Question I want listeners to answer:**
