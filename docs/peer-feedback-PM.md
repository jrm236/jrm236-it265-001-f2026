# IT265 Module 2: Peer Feedback

Use one copy for each concept you review. Keep actual workshop notes together in this document, including feedback you received when available. Initials are enough; do not record classmates' contact details. If feedback was unavailable, briefly say so instead of inventing it. Give feedback on the idea and name a decision the designer can make next.

**Concept:** Paralympic Minigames

**Reviewer initials:** J.M

**In my words, the player repeatedly:** Draw cards every round, choose a combination of restriction or strategy cards to use, then compete in WarioWare-like minigames where cards affect how easy or difficult the challenge can be.

**One clarifying question:** How are minigames decided on every round? Additionally, what happens when you run out of cards if they are all either in use or previously used?

**Strongest hook or source of appeal:** I would say the strongest appeal would be doing the minigames with the restrictions. It seems like this game would be a pretty fun party game.

**What one student could prototype first:** For the first prototype, you could just do some simple restrictions that don't require any outside items and use handwritten cards.

**Biggest uncertainty or risk to test:** I would say the biggest risk is making the challenges still possible under the restrictions, as games might progress too quickly if players can't win with specific restrictions.

**One actionable suggestion:** You should probably test out all of the possible combinations with a few minigames to see which ones are still possible with the restriction cards and select those minigames to be a part of the main pool.

Give your notes to the designer. Designers can use the feedback in the [selection sheet](./04-select-and-scope.html) and journal entry. Link this document's rendered page from your workshop index.
