# IT265 Module 2: Peer Feedback

Use one copy for each concept you review. Keep actual workshop notes together in this document, including feedback you received when available. Initials are enough; do not record classmates' contact details. If feedback was unavailable, briefly say so instead of inventing it. Give feedback on the idea and name a decision the designer can make next.

**Concept:** Grid-Tactics Game

**Reviewer initials:** A.S

**In my words, the player repeatedly:** Completes 3 actions, and may move, attack, or use a special faction ability.

**One clarifying question:** If a player wants to spend a turn without an action, are they allowed to?

**Strongest hook or source of appeal:** Similar to how some games like Overwatch or Valorant has ultimates, I'd say the use of faction abilities--being non-regenerating--may feel special, or meant to be used strategically to turn the tide of battle, which can lead to really well thought out planning and come off as extremely satisfying to play when done correctly.

**What one student could prototype first:** For the first prototype, maybe check if a 5x5 is enough space. If a player uses their 3 turns to move 1 character 3 times, that character would already be at the other side of the board in 2 loops.

**Biggest uncertainty or risk to test:** I'd say the biggest risk here is balancing faction abilities. Since we don't know how they might work, an off balance game may slip very quickly into an unbeatable meta.


**One actionable suggestion:**  I'd say start with working out the faction abilities. Then build more general or shared movesets off those.
