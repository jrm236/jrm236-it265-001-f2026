# IT265 Module 2: Concept Pitch Cards

Copy this card for each of your two or three shortlisted concepts. Aim to explain each in about one minute. Use your [idea bank](./01-idea-bank.html) as a starting point.

## Pitch Card

**Working title:** Worlds Worst Wizards

**Player role and situation:** The players are the world's most incompetent wizards dueling one another to see who is the best.

**Repeated decision or action:** Players draw cards every round, comprised of different words or sentences with blank lines. They can combine the cards to create the name and the description for the spell.

**Goal, pressure, or ending:** Every round, the players vote for who they believe has the best card. After a certain number of turns, the player who has the highest amount of points wins.

**Hook:** Cards can be used to create unusual and comical effects, making it fun as a party game.

**Smallest useful physical prototype:** The smallest useful prototype for this game could use paper to handwrite each card. Additionally, to keep track of points, the first physical prototype could use coins.

**Question I want listeners to answer:** Would the current way cards work be flexible enough to make new and funny spells constantly.

Keep the cards together in this document and link its rendered page from your workshop index. They support the journal and treatment rather than adding another graded submission.
