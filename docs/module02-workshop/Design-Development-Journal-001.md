## Concept and Decision

**Current concept and intended experience:** The current concept is to create a competitive tactics game that takes place on a 5x5 grid, where players get to choose between multiple unique factions and control three of their units against another player. Both players get three action points per turn that can be used to activate their units, allowing them to move and attack. Each faction also has a unique ability that can be activated once per turn using a special faction ability point that doesn't regenerate for the remainder of the match. The intended experience is to have a relatively short tactics game where players must carefully decide what to do for that round, while also being able to have their own unique loadout.

**Design question or decision:** The main design decision is whether the action point activation system and the 5x5 grid provide meaningful choices while still being relatively balanced for all factions.

**Selected concept and alternatives kept:** The concept I selected was the Grid-Based Tactics game. I have also decided to keep the other two concepts as alternatives in case this idea proves too complicated.
